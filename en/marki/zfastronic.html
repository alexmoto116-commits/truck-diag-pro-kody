How to read the number

ZF AS-Tronic numbers its faults its own way, not by the J1939 SPN standard: the first part is the fault number, after the dot — its type. The same number on an engine or another make means something else.

Know the code number? Type it into the search — or paste the whole list from the scanner and we will show which fault is the real one.
