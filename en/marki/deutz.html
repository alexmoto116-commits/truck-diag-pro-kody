The database covers 1057 dealer codes — Deutz uses its own factory format, outside the J1939 SPN/FMI standard. A few examples below; look up your own code in the search on the homepage, where you can also pick “Deutz” from the brand list.

Deutz codes: what to know

Deutz has a dealer table: 1057 codes. The most common areas are the exhaust and AdBlue system (SCR), fuel and intake air. Codes such as 100.0 and 100.1 look like SPN and FMI, but the Deutz table has its own scheme, so take the meaning from the description. For example, 100.0 is critical overpressure of the engine oil.
