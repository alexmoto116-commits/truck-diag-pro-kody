Renault Trucks codes: what to know

Renault Trucks in the free tables: 296 codes under J1939, one of the fuller SPN tables on the site. The most common areas are fuel, brakes and the power supply. Brake codes are listed separately, and if ABS codes are in the reading, start with the brakes rather than the engine. Renault has 91 codes in the dealer format, mostly with letter codes such as IND103.

How to read a code

A code has two halves. SPN is what is actually at fault — the sensor, component, or parameter. FMI is what is wrong with it — value out of range, open circuit, short circuit, invalid data. So “SPN 100” alone is not a diagnosis yet, but “100/1” already tells you oil pressure has dropped below normal.

If there are several codes at once

That is almost always how it goes: one fault drags five or six codes behind it. An empty AdBlue tank first throws a level error, then interrupted dosing, then a NOx excess and a torque derate.
