Webasto fault codes

The database covers 55 dealer codes — Webasto uses its own factory format, outside the J1939 SPN/FMI standard. A few examples below; look up your own code in the search on the homepage, where you can also pick “Webasto” from the brand list.

Webasto codes: what to know

Webasto is a heater: 55 codes: PC codes of the Webasto Thermo Test program such as 00, 01 and 02, and timer F-codes F00-F19. These are two different systems; the match between them is given in each entry. As with Eberspächer, the zero row means "no faults", so look for a code with a non-zero number.
