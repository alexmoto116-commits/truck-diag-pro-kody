Volvo (MID modules) codes: what to know

A Volvo code here has three parts: the MID is the unit that logged the fault, the PID/SID is the parameter or component, and the FMI is the fault type. There are 1820 codes, and the most common areas are the power supply, the CAN bus and exhaust. To find a code, pick the unit by its MID first, then the parameter and the FMI inside it. A form such as M128-P100.1 shows all three parts at once.

Volvo (MID modules) code examples

M128-P100.1Engine oil pressure. The oil level is low. The oil is thin, thick or dirty. The bypass valves, the oil pump or the oil pressure sensor may be faulty.
M128-PP326.0Soot level. The data is valid but above normal
M128-PS72.4Thermostat bypass valve. Short circuit to a low-voltage circuit, or low voltage
M128-S5.11Injector, cylinder 5. Intermittent faults. High cylinder balance data, faulty injector, low compression. Reduced power, uneven running, possible noise.
M130-PS5.5Ground of the fast engagement and disengagement PWM valves. An open circuit or a short to the supply in the clutch cylinder circuit.
M136-PS52.10Yaw rate sensor. The yaw rate sensor is faulty, the sensor is fitted incorrectly, the wheelbase or track value is wrong, the speed calibration value does not match the wheel speed. Check that the yaw rate sensor is fitted correctly, check the vehicle speed calibration data. Try restart no. 4.
M136-S12.14Modulator, axle 3, right. Modulator or brake chamber lines damaged, leaking or blocked, insufficient air at the modulator, faulty modulator, blocked modulator exhaust. Check air lines, modulator exhaust and inlet air pressure. Try restart No. 1.
M136-S6.8Wheel speed sensor, axle 3, right. Sensor signal shorted to another signal. Check wires and sensor resistance; if fine, replace the modulator.
M140-PS210.12Loss of data link communication with the exterior lighting control unit (LCM MID216). The data link wiring is faulty, or the connector contacts are damaged.
M144-PS227.9Loss of SAE J1939 communication with the air dryer control unit (MID185). For 5 seconds the vehicle control unit (MID144) has received no messages over the SAE J1939 data link from the air dryer control unit (MID185). The data link wires between the units are broken or partly damaged.
M150-PS9.7Solenoid valve, auxiliary axle (TRIDEM). Solenoid valve does not fill or empty the air springs, and the air spring pressure sensor shows no change. Valve internals seized, heavy air leak, or faulty pressure sensor.
M184-PS208.0Loss of SAE J1939 communication with the air suspension control unit (MID150). The load signal from the air suspension pressure regulator is above the permissible value. An implausible signal from the air suspension control unit (MID150). Check the fault codes of the air suspension control unit.

M222-S250.9SAE J1708 data link. Several data link messages were missed. The retarder control unit is being programmed, the data link wiring is damaged, or the connector contacts are corroded.
