Volvo (MID modules) codes: what to know

A Volvo code here has three parts: the MID is the unit that logged the fault, the PID/SID is the parameter or component, and the FMI is the fault type. There are 1800 codes, and the most common areas are the power supply, the CAN bus and exhaust. To find a code, pick the unit by its MID first, then the parameter and the FMI inside it. A form such as M128-P100.1 shows all three parts at once.

Volvo (MID modules) code examples

M128-P100.1Engine oil pressure. The oil level is low. The oil is thin, thick or dirty. The bypass valves, the oil pump or the oil pressure sensor may be faulty.
M128-PP278.5SCR urea level. Open circuit, or low current
M128-PS72.1Thermostat bypass valve. The data is valid but below normal
M128-S42.3Fuel distributor. A short to the supply in the wiring or inside the distributor.
M130-PS30.9TMS data link. The TMS link is similar to the SAE J1939 data link and is used only for data exchange between the transmission control unit (MID130) and the gear selector control unit (MID223). The data is updated at a low rate. The selector control unit (MID223) has no power supply. The selector control unit (MID223) is in programming mode. Damaged data link wires or corroded connector contacts. There may be a fault in the control unit.
M136-PS50.3Stability control (ESP) control unit. The wires between the sensor and the ESP unit are shorted to negative or to positive, the ESP unit is faulty, the connector contacts between the ESP unit and the EBS unit or between the ESP unit and the sensor are damaged. Check the wiring and the connector contacts of the motion sensors and of the steered wheel angle sensor. Check the ESP unit.
M136-S12.10Modulator, axle 3, right. Faulty modulator; modulator power cut until internal operations finish (for example battery disconnected with ignition on). Clear fault codes; if the fault recurs, replace the modulator.
M136-S6.3Wheel speed sensor, axle 3, right. One of the wires shorted to positive. Check the sensor wiring and connector.
M140-PS227.12Loss of data link communication with the air dryer unit (APM MID185). The data link wiring is faulty, or the connector contacts are damaged.
M144-PS230.4Software fault. An incorrect software restart. Power interruptions, a software failure, or strong electromagnetic interference.
M150-S1.7Solenoid valve, front axle air suspension. Mechanical solenoid valve fault: valve components seized open or closed. Before replacing the valve, check for air leaks and the front axle air spring pressure sensor readings.
M184-PS3.5Proportional valve (located in the control valve unit). The valve is supplied with a voltage below the permissible values. The wiring is shorted to ground.
M214-PS7.12Siren. The alarm control unit detected a fault in the siren. The siren failed because of mechanical damage or environmental influence. The battery inside the siren may be in poor condition.
M216-S250.12SAE J1708 data link. The data link is faulty inside the engine control unit. Internal fault in the unit. Replace the control unit.
M223-P158.0Control unit supply voltage. The GECU unit supply voltage is above 33 V. A fast battery charger is connected, welding work was carried out, or the battery charging regulator is faulty.
