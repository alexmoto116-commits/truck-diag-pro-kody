Webasto heater error 88

Code 88, Webasto heater. Factory text below; then what to check and the neighbouring codes.

What the code means

Fuel pump circuit interrupted

The fuel pump feeds diesel from the tank to the heater. The fault concerns the pump or its circuit: the pump does not run or runs incorrectly, or its supply circuit is open or shorted.

Why it appears

The first cause is the most common; the rest follow roughly in order of likelihood:

  1. A broken or chafed pump wire, at the connector or under a bracket.
  2. A short to earth in the pump circuit.
  3. A blocked heater fuel filter or line, so the pump runs almost dry.
  4. Wear of the pump itself.

Steps to take

  1. Write down the code, when it appeared and the conditions: cold start, driving or idling, and the vehicle voltage.
  2. Switch the heater off from the panel and wait until the shutdown cycle ends, with the fan and fuel pump stopped.
  3. Try one restart. If the code returns, go through the checks below, but do not keep restarting one after another.

What to check yourself

Simple checks, no dismantling:

  • Check the pump connector for corrosion, looseness and moisture.
  • Inspect the pump wire along its whole run, especially at the fixing points.
  • Inspect the filter and line for blockage and air being drawn in.
  • Check that the tank has clean fuel without water.
  • Heater fuse: find it on the schematic or in the make's manual and look at it. Replace it only with a fuse of the same rating.
  • Battery: terminals and earth are tight and clean, and the battery holds voltage during start-up.
  • Fuel: the tank has diesel, it suits the season and has no water, and the filter is not clogged.
  • Air and exhaust: the air intake and exhaust pipe are free of snow, ice, mud and crushing.
  • Connectors: unplug and reconnect the heater and the faulty part's connector, check the contacts and clean any corrosion without bending the pins.

When you need a service

If the connector, wire and filter are sound but the code remains, a service checks the pump and its circuit.

Go to a service if the code returns after two or three simple checks, if there are fuel leaks, soot on the housing or exhaust, if the heater does not work at all, or if the code points to the control unit.

Can you drive and sleep with it

You can usually drive with a faulty heater, because it does not affect steering or brakes. Do not run it if there is a smell of fuel or burning, smoke, or visible flame outside, and never leave it running unattended.

Sleeping in a cold cab without heat is dangerous, because hypothermia sets in unnoticed, especially when tired. Take a sleeping bag and warm clothes for the night. If you need the engine running at a stop, follow the safety rules and do not bring gas or household heaters into the cab.

Why it matters

The danger is not the code number itself but smoke, a smell of fuel and an overheating housing. If you see these signs, switch the heater off and do not restart it until it has been inspected. A faulty combustion chamber or a blocked exhaust can let carbon monoxide into the cab, so do not touch or block the exhaust while driving.
