MID 128 PID 105: intake manifold temperature - FMI list

PID 105 in SAE J1587 is intake manifold temperature. MID 128 is the engine. Volvo table: FMI 0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 10.

Can you drive

Drive carefully

You can usually drive: when the reading is lost, the engine ECU substitutes a fallback value and may reduce power a little. Stop if power drops sharply, consumption jumps or smoke appears.

The FMIs listed are those the Volvo table gives for this part. The full FMI list is in the section index.

What to check, in order

  1. FMI 0: temperature above normal. Check the intercooler: dirt on the cores, oil seepage, a loose pipe.
  2. Check the engine is not overheating and the radiator next to the intercooler is not blocked.
  3. FMI 4 and 5: check the sensor connector and wire (short to ground or an open circuit).
  4. FMI 2 and 10: unstable readings point to a poor sensor contact or corroded connector.
  5. Compare with other boost codes (PID 102).

When a workshop is needed

The code stays after cleaning the intercooler and connector: replace the sensor and check the circuit in a workshop.

Questions and answers

Why is MID 128 PID 105 FMI 0 harmful?

Hot intake air lowers power and overloads the engine if the cause is a blocked intercooler.

Is the intake air temperature sensor expensive?

It is usually a simple sensor; check the connector and wire first, then replace.
