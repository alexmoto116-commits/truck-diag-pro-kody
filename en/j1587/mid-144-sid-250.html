MID 144 SID 250 FMI 2: vehicle ECU J1587/J1708 link

SID 250 in SAE J1587 is the J1708/J1587 data link. MID 144 is the vehicle ECU at Volvo (cruise control in the SAE standard). Volvo table: FMI 2, erratic, intermittent or incorrect data.

Can you drive

You can drive

The backup bus is unstable; the main link runs on J1939. You can drive if there are no J1939 (SID 231) codes and everything works.

What to check, in order

  1. Check the vehicle ECU connectors and the diagnostic connector.
  2. Unplug add-on devices on the diagnostic connector (trackers, recorders) and see whether the code goes.
  3. Inspect the J1708 line: opens, chafing.
  4. Check ground and supply at the vehicle ECU.
  5. Compare with MID 144 SID 231.

When a workshop is needed

The code stays with no add-on devices: bus diagnosis in a workshop.

Where the sources differ

In the SAE J1587 standard MID 144 is cruise control; in Volvo tables the unit is the Vehicle ECU (VECU), and the VNL manual labels it "Cab Controller". Use the meaning for your make.

Questions and answers

Is MID 144 SID 250 FMI 2 dangerous?

By itself no: J1708 is the backup bus.

Which devices interfere most often?

Trackers and recorders connected to the diagnostic connector.
