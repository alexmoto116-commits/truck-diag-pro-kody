MID 144 SID 231 FMI 2 and 12: vehicle ECU J1939 link fault

SID 231 is the SAE J1939 link (by the standard); MID 144 is the Vehicle ECU (VECU) at Volvo and cruise control in the SAE standard. The Volvo manual describes "SAE J1939 Control Link": the fast link between units that controls the truck; if it fails, J1587/J1708 serves as the backup.

Can you drive

You can drive, carefully

Volvo manual: the code turns on the yellow lamp. You can drive, but cruise control, gearbox and other functions may be limited. A red lamp, power loss or an engine stop means stopping.

What to check, in order

  1. FMI 2: the ECU gets no confirmation for messages on J1939. The Volvo manual lists the causes: a loose connection, an open J1939 wire, contact resistance and oxidation.
  2. FMI 12: expected messages from some unit on the bus are missing. The same causes: an open wire, contact oxidation.
  3. Open and inspect the connectors of the units on J1939, especially the vehicle ECU, engine, gearbox and brake ECU.
  4. Test the J1939 harness continuity and the resistance between CAN High and CAN Low (about 60 ohm with power off).
  5. Look at MID 144 PSID 200-214: "Communication Error, J1939" with a unit number shows which unit dropped out.

When a workshop is needed

You cannot find the unit or the code returns after cleaning the contacts: bus diagnosis in a workshop (Volvo: the "SAE J1939 Control Link, Check" procedure).

Where the sources differ

In the SAE J1587 standard MID 144 is cruise control; in Volvo tables the unit is the Vehicle ECU (VECU), and the VNL manual labels it "Cab Controller". Use the meaning for your make.

Questions and answers

Is MID 144 at Volvo cruise control?

In the SAE J1587 standard MID 144 is cruise control. In Volvo tables the unit is the Vehicle ECU (VECU), and the VNL manual labels it "Cab Controller". Use the description for your make.

What is the difference between FMI 2 and FMI 12 for SID 231?

FMI 2: the ECU gets no confirmation of its own messages. FMI 12: expected messages from some unit on the bus do not arrive.
