Auxiliary heater faults on VAG cars in VCDS and ODIS

16 pages

If an auxiliary heater (Webasto, Eberspacher) is fitted to a VAG group vehicle, its control unit appears in VCDS and ODIS as address 18, Aux. Heat (sometimes 0B, Sec. Air Heating, for an air heater). Faults are stored as five-digit codes (0xxxx) or, on newer vehicles, short four-digit ones. The heater does not affect steering or brakes, so you can almost always drive; the task is to find the cause and not to leave the heater on if you smell fuel or see smoke.
