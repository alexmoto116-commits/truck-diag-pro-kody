VCDS 02251 heater unit locked: how to unlock the heater

Code 02251: the heater control unit locked the heater after other faults (overheating, no flame). Until the cause is found and the lock released, the heater will not start and does not clear codes.

Can you drive

You can drive, the heater is off

You can drive. The lock protects the heater and is not dangerous itself, but there is usually another cause beneath it (low fuel, overheating, no flame).

What the code means

CodeName
02251Heater unit locked

Address 18 in VCDS is called Aux. Heat (auxiliary heater).

Possible causes

  • Ross-Tech: a possible cause is a low fuel level.
  • The lock occurs after system codes in address 18; for example per TPI 2016660 three occurrences of 01412 give 02251 (Ross-Tech).
  • Forum threads: the code appears after 01410 (overheating) and after failed starts.

What to check, in order

  1. First read the other codes in address 18 and fix them: the lock returns while the cause lives.
  2. Unlock by saving "1" in an adaptation channel of address 18 (Ross-Tech): Touareg 7L and Transporter 7H channel 003; Touran 1T and similar 042; Audi A8 4E and Q7 4L 007; Audi A4 8K, A5 8T, Q5 8R, A6 4F/4G, A7 4G, A8 4H 042. Then clear the codes.
  3. T6 (per t6forum): through the T6 Basic Settings "unlock" (IDE06654), then clear the remaining codes.
  4. Another forum method: pull the heater fuse for at least 10 seconds (not for every lockout).
  5. Check the fuel in the tank.

When a workshop is needed

The lock returns: do not keep unlocking; find the cause (bleeding, pump, overheating) in a workshop.

Questions and answers

What does VCDS 02251 mean?

The heater is locked because of previously stored faults.

Do the sources differ?

Ross-Tech gives adaptation channels per model, t6forum gives other ways (T6 Basic Settings, a fuse pull, Webasto Thermo Test software). Start with the Ross-Tech method for your model.
