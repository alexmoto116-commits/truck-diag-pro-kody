Diesel particulate filter (DPF): what the lamps and codes mean, when you can drive

In short. On a steady amber DPF lamp you can drive, but regeneration has to be arranged straight away. A flashing lamp together with check engine cannot wait: a parked regeneration is needed. A red STOP lamp with DPF means the vehicle must not be operated.

Text of the video

The video is in Russian; below is an English translation of the narration.

SPN 3719 - the particulate filter is accumulating soot and clogging. There are three levels: FMI 15, 16 and 0. The lamp first stays on steadily, then flashes, then turns red. While the lamp is steady and power has not dropped, finish the trip and let the filter regenerate. If the lamp flashes or is red, or power has dropped, stop, run a parked regeneration following the manufacturer instructions and do not interrupt it. If that does not help, go to a workshop: regeneration does not burn off ash. The decision to drive is the driver's.

How the filter cleans itself

The Cummins manual describes three ways:

  • passive regeneration: while driving, when the exhaust is hot enough on its own and the unit does nothing;
  • active: the unit lacks heat, injects a little unburnt fuel into the stream and ignites the soot;
  • forced (parked): the vehicle stands, revs are raised, the filter cleans for up to an hour; started with a button or from a menu if a highway run did not help or the lamp flashes.

Short trips and cold exhaust get in the way, which is why the lamp comes on more often in city driving.

Main SPN codes

SPNMeaning
3719DPF soot load, in percent. FMI 15, 16, 0 are three levels, from low to most severe
3251DPF differential pressure - how clogged the filter is. FMI 15, 16, 0: above normal; FMI 3 and 4: sensor circuit; FMI 2: unstable signal
3697DPF lamp command on the dashboard
3698High exhaust temperature lamp command (HEST)
3703Active regeneration inhibited by the inhibit switch (FMI 31: condition exists)
3695 and 3696Switches: regeneration inhibit and forced regeneration

SPN 3719 and 3251 are linked: the unit judges clogging by the differential pressure and then computes the soot load. Numbers 3700-3702 (regeneration status) differ between sources, so they are left out of the table. Code page for the differential pressure: SPN 3251.

Which lamp demands what

On the dashboardWhat to do
DPF steadyThe filter needs regeneration. Make sure the inhibit switch is off. At the first chance get onto a highway and keep going for 20-30 minutes. The Actros gives a window on a steady amber lamp: first four hours, then 30 minutes
DPF flashingRegeneration is urgent, power may drop automatically. On the Actros a flashing amber lamp means start regeneration manually at once
DPF flashing and check engine onImmediate parked regeneration is needed, power is cut harder; "I will make it home" is no longer an option
Red STOP lamp together with DPFThe vehicle must not be operated: stop and call a workshop
HEST (high exhaust temperature)Regeneration is under way and the exhaust is hot. Not a fault; do not point the pipe at flammable objects

Parked regeneration, step by step

Per the Cummins driver card (on an Actros the start procedure is in the on-board manual):

  1. Park on level ground with nothing near the exhaust.
  2. Parking brake on, gearbox in neutral or Park.
  3. Fast idle and power take-off (PTO) off.
  4. Press the manual regeneration switch.
  5. Revs will rise and the turbo sound will change; this is normal. Regeneration takes up to an hour.
  6. Stay by the vehicle and watch the exhaust. If it becomes unsafe, shut off the engine or press the brake or accelerator pedal: that interrupts regeneration.
  7. After it ends the exhaust and surfaces stay hot for another 3-5 minutes.

What not to do

  • interrupt an active regeneration without need;
  • start a forced regeneration without finding the cause: otherwise the vehicle will be back with the same problem in a week;
  • keep the inhibit switch on without reason: per Cummins this leads to frequent parked regenerations and a clogged filter;
  • expect regeneration to clear ash: ash does not burn, it is removed by cleaning or replacing the filter;
  • replace the filter for SPN 3251 without checking the sensor tubes, connector and the sensor itself.

See a workshop if regeneration does not run and the lamp stays on, a red STOP lamp is on with DPF, SPN 3251 with FMI 0 and 16 remains after regeneration, or regenerations are needed more and more often. Related pages: clogged particulate filter and regeneration will not run.

Questions and answers

Can I drive with the amber DPF lamp on?

Yes, if it is steady, but regeneration must be done at once: get onto a highway and keep going for at least 20-30 minutes. A flashing lamp with check engine cannot wait.

How long does a parked regeneration take?

Up to an hour. Stay by the vehicle and watch the exhaust the whole time; afterwards it stays hot for 3-5 minutes.

Why does regeneration not remove everything from the filter?

Soot burns off, ash does not. Ash is removed by cleaning or replacing the filter.

How is SPN 3719 different from SPN 3251?

SPN 3251 is the differential pressure across the filter, from which the unit judges clogging. SPN 3719 is the computed soot load in percent.
