Webasto: why service code 03 and F03 are different things

In short. Webasto has two code systems: F00-F15 on the timer and hexadecimal service codes in the workshop software. The digits coincide, the meaning does not: service 03 is flame-out, F03 is voltage. A driver needs the F-code and the model of the heater.

Webasto, F01 - the heater does not start. Can you keep driving? Yes: this is the heater, not the engine. The code means no start: no flame appeared even after a repeat attempt. Where the confusion comes from

One F-code on the timer groups several service codes. On the Air Top 2000 ST service code 03 is flame-out, which the timer shows as F02. F03 is a voltage problem, with service codes 84 and 04. The Thermo Top Evo table has no service code 03 at all.

F03 on the Thermo Top Evo is also about the on-board voltage. The repair table labels it "too high", the user manual "too low or raised"; for the driver it is one thing: check the supply voltage. The conclusion: read an F-code only together with the heater model.

How the driver sees the code

  • Combined timer on the Air Top 2000 ST: a code number on the screen until the fault is cleared; the manual advises contacting a Webasto service.
  • ON indicator without a display: 5 seconds of rapid flashing, then the number of long flashes equals the F-code number (F04 is four long flashes). Plain rapid flashing is F00.
  • MultiControl and SmartControl on the Thermo Top Evo: F01-F15 on the display, while F16-F19 show as "- -". Codes of the form TXX belong to the controller itself.
  • W-bus and analog connection on the Thermo Top Evo: hexadecimal Hxx numbers over W-bus, Fxx over analog.

Telestart and ThermoCall controllers show neither the F-code number nor the fact of a heater fault, only the state of the link, battery and module. ThermoCall has a general warning (the heater does not execute the command, service needed). The F-code comes from the heater itself or the indicator in the cab.

What you can do and when to see a workshop

  • identify the heater model and the type of controller;
  • for F03, inspect the terminals and charge the battery;
  • do not compare the number with the service-code table: use the table for your model.

Factory manuals leave the driver little to do. The Webasto instruction directly advises contacting a service for any number code on the timer. Diagnostics are needed for F00, F07, F08, F09, F10 and F11: the control unit, pump, drive, glow plug and overheating.

Questions and answers

Are service code 03 and F03 the same?

No. On the Air Top 2000 ST service 03 is flame-out, which the timer shows as F02. F03 is the on-board voltage.

Why does the same F-code mean something else on the Thermo Top Evo?

The code tables differ between models. For example F11 is the overheat sensor on the Air Top 2000 ST and the coolant pump on the Thermo Top Evo.

Will a Telestart or ThermoCall controller show an F-code?

No, they show the link, battery charge and module state. The F-code comes from the heater or its indicator.

What does the number of long flashes on the ON indicator equal?

The F-code number: after 5 seconds of rapid flashing, four long flashes are F04. Plain rapid flashing means F00.
