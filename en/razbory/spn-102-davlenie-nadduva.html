SPN 102: boost out of range - whistling, smoke, oil on the pipes

In short. If power has dropped but there is no whistle, smoke or oil on the pipes, you can usually get to base without load. Whistling, smoke or oil on the pipes: stop, a boost leak or a faulty turbo will not clear by itself.

SPN 102 - boost pressure. Can you keep driving? This is the air pressure in the intake manifold. FMI 16 - boost above normal, FMI 18 - below normal. You can drive, but not far: on a number of engines the ECU limits power by itself. Whistling, smoke or oil on the hoses - stop.

What the code means

SPN 102 in J1939 is Engine intake manifold 1 pressure, i.e. the boost from the turbocharger.

FMIMeaningWhat to do
16Boost above normal, medium urgency (Detroit DD13/DD15: "over-boost detected"; Cummins L9: FC 124)Check the turbo wastegate and the sensor
18Boost below normal, medium urgency (Detroit: "lower than expected under load"; Cummins L9: FC 125)Look for an air leak and a blocked path
2Data erratic or incorrect (Cummins ISB 6.7: FC 2973)Check the sensor and connector
3Sensor circuit voltage above normal or short to positive (Cummins ISB 6.7: FC 122)Check connector and wiring
4Sensor circuit voltage below normal or short to ground (Cummins ISB 6.7: FC 123)Check connector and wiring

FMI 16 and 18 speak of the pressure itself, FMI 2, 3 and 4 of the sensor and its circuit. Until the reading is verified, treat the boost as real. Thresholds differ between engines.

Can you drive

The engine's reaction depends on the manufacturer. Per Foton and SITRAK factory tables, torque is limited and black smoke appears with FMI 16 and 18. On the Detroit DD13 and DD15 the same codes only light the lamps and the engine does not react.

  • power dropped but there is no whistle, smoke or oil on the pipes: get to base without extra load and look for the cause at once;
  • you hear a whistle, see smoke or oil on the pipes: stop.

What to inspect

  1. In a safe place inspect the pipes and clamps between the turbo, intercooler and intake manifold. Under load a leak is heard as a whistle.
  2. Check the intercooler for cracks and oil, the air filter for clogging.
  3. Look at the turbo actuator: the wastegate and its tubes. FMI 16 occurs when the valve does not open or the variable geometry is stuck.
  4. With the engine off and ignition on, compare the sensor reading with atmospheric pressure. On a Detroit the allowed difference is up to 10.3 kPa (1.5 psi); more, and you inspect the connector and replace the sensor.
  5. If there is oil on the pipes or the sensor port is clogged with carbon, remove and clean it, and look for the cause in the turbo.

When to see a workshop

  • whistle, smoke or oil on the pipes: repair of the air path or the turbo;
  • the code repeats after inspecting the pipes, intercooler and turbo actuator: turbo and valve diagnostics;
  • FMI 2, 3 or 4 repeats after the connector and wiring were checked: diagnostics of the sensor and its 5 V supply line.

Questions and answers

Can I drive with SPN 102?

If power has dropped but there is no whistle, smoke or oil on the pipes, you can get to base without extra load. With a whistle, smoke or oil, stop.

How is FMI 16 different from FMI 18?

FMI 16 is boost above normal (over-boost), FMI 18 below normal. Both are medium urgency.

What do FMI 2, 3 and 4 mean for SPN 102?

A fault of the sensor or its circuit, not necessarily of the turbo: check the connector and wiring first.

How do I check the pressure sensor?

With the engine off and ignition on, compare its reading with atmospheric pressure; on a Detroit the allowed difference is up to 10.3 kPa (1.5 psi).
