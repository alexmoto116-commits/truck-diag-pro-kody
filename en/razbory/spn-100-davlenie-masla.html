SPN 100: oil pressure has dropped - shut the engine off

In short. With FMI 1 and 18 the oil pressure really is below normal and the engine must be shut off at once: without oil the crankshaft and bearings wear out within minutes. FMI 3 and 4 concern the sensor, but until the pressure is checked with a mechanical gauge, treat it as real.

FMI breakdown

SPN 100 in the J1939 standard is Engine oil pressure.

FMIMeaningWhat to do
1Pressure below normal, most severe level (Cummins ISB 6.7: FC 415)Shut off the engine
18Pressure below normal, medium urgency (Cummins ISB 6.7: FC 143)Do not drive, find the cause
3Sensor circuit voltage above normal or short to positive (Cummins FC 135)Check connector and wiring
4Sensor circuit voltage below normal or short to ground (Cummins FC 141)Check connector and wiring

FMI 1 and 18 speak of the pressure itself, FMI 3 and 4 of the sensor's electrical circuit.

Can you drive

No. With FMI 1 and 18 shut the engine off at once and do not push on "to the nearest place": oil starvation destroys the crankshaft and bearings within minutes. With FMI 3 and 4 first check whether the sensor is lying by fitting a mechanical gauge.

What the driver does

  1. Stop and shut off the engine.
  2. Wait a few minutes and check the oil level on the dipstick on level ground.
  3. Inspect the engine for oil leaks.
  4. Level is fine: screw a mechanical gauge in place of the sensor and compare with the dashboard.
  5. Gauge shows normal: the sensor or wiring is at fault, so inspect the connector and wires. No pressure: do not start the engine, call a tow truck.

When to see a workshop

  • pressure is really low with a normal oil level: a clogged pickup screen, a worn oil pump or bearings, fuel or coolant in the oil are possible;
  • FMI 3 or 4 repeats after the connector and wiring were checked: sensor and sensor supply line diagnostics are needed.

Questions and answers

Can I drive with SPN 100 FMI 1?

No. Shut the engine off at once: without oil pressure the crankshaft and bearings wear out within minutes.

How is FMI 18 different from FMI 1?

Both mean pressure below normal. FMI 1 is the most severe level, FMI 18 medium urgency; in both cases you must not drive and must find the cause.

What do FMI 3 and 4 mean for SPN 100?

A fault in the sensor circuit (voltage above normal or short to positive, below normal or short to ground), not proof that the oil is low.

How do I confirm the pressure is really low?

Screw a mechanical gauge in place of the sensor and compare the reading with the dashboard.
