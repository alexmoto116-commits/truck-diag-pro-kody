Can I keep driving

Do not drive. Stop as soon as it’s safe to do so — don’t keep going.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: Minutes

The oil film in the bearings is already breaking down. Every minute under load is metal on metal on the crank journals.

Next comes spun bearings and a full engine rebuild. Shut it down now and coast to the shoulder without revving.

The pressure itself is most likely fine: this is an electrical fault — an open or a short in the sensor circuit, not lost oil. You can drive, but the dash will no longer warn you about a real pressure drop. Until the circuit is repaired, check the level with the dipstick at every stop.

What to check on the spot

Shut the engine down. Check the oil level with the dipstick and look underneath for leaks. Don’t restart until pressure is back.
