SPN 3222 — Aftertreatment 1 Intake Gas Sensor Heater

Do not driveTo the nearest safe stop

J1939 code SPN 3222. Below — how the fault is worded at the factory by Cummins ISB, KAMAZ-5490 and SITRAK, and what the second half of the code (FMI) means in the standard.

How to check it yourself

Heater of the oxygen sensor at the catalyst inlet. The heater lets the sensor reach its working temperature quickly. The code refers to the heater circuit and to the resistance of the sensor heating element.

What it should read

Heater resistance with a cold sensorper the manufacturer's manual
Sensor heating after start-uphappens within the time set by the unit — check by diagnostics

Check order

  1. FMI 0, 1, 2 (heater signal above or below the working range, or invalid): unplug the heater connector, inspect the contacts, and with the ignition on measure the voltage on the contacts.
  2. FMI 3 (high armature voltage of the UEGO sensor): check the heater wire for a short to plus.
  3. FMI 10 (slow heating of the heating element): check the heater resistance and the supply; slow heating points to the sensor.

Common causes — from most to least frequent

  1. Corroded sensor connector.
  2. Broken or shorted heater wires.
  3. Worn sensor or its heater.
  4. Heater supply from the control unit.

Can I keep driving

Do not drive. Stop as soon as it’s safe to do so — don’t keep going.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: To the nearest safe stop

The code is flagged critical: the manufacturer considers it unsafe to keep driving without checking.

This code alone cannot say what will fail next, so stop at the first safe opportunity and look at it on the spot.
