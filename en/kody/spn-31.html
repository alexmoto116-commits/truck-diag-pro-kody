What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: To the nearest safe stop

The gearbox or clutch has gone into limp mode: some gears may be unavailable and an automated box may stop shifting under load.

The main risk is being left on a climb with no gear and rolling back. Do not take a loaded truck into the hills; reaching level ground and a shop is fine.

That said, the manufacturer flags this code as critical — if in doubt, it is safer to stop and check on the spot.
