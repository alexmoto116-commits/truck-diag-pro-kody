SPN 35 — factory code Ford Trucks and ZF AS-Tronic

SPN 35 is a manufacturer number outside the standard J1939 table: different makes can put different things under it. Below — how it is worded by Ford Trucks and ZF AS-Tronic, and what the second half of the code (FMI) means.

Ford Trucks

CodeMeaning
FMI 0Urea Tank Temperature Sensor Electrical – On / Off Power

ZF AS-Tronic

CodeMeaning
FMI 10Fault on valve Y3 output circuit (front splitter group). With a direct-drive (DD) gearbox, shifting into the fast group is not possible. With an overdrive (OD) gearbox, shifting into the slow group is not possible.

Can I keep driving

Do not drive. Stop as soon as it’s safe to do so — don’t keep going.
