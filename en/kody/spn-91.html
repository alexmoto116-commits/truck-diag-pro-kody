SPN 91 — Accelerator pedal position

Do not driveTo the nearest safe stop

J1939 code SPN 91. Below — how the fault is worded at the factory by Cummins ISB, DAF, Dongfeng, Foton, Isuzu, KAMAZ, KAMAZ-5490, MAZ, Mercedes-Benz, Renault Trucks, SITRAK, YaMZ and ZF AS-Tronic, and what the second half of the code (FMI) means in the standard.

How to check it yourself

The accelerator pedal sensor sits right on the pedal. It has two independent channels inside: the second (SPN 29) lets the ECU catch an error in the first. Many pedals also have an idle switch (SPN 558).

What it should read

Reference voltage at the connector, ignition on5.0 V ± 0.25
First channel: pedal released — pressed to the floorusually about 0.5…1 V — 3.5…4.5 V
Second channelon many pedals half of the first — per the factory data

Check order

  1. In diagnostics watch the pedal position in percent: released — 0, floor — 100, and smoothly, without dropouts when pressing slowly.
  2. Dropouts when pressing slowly — the resistive track is worn: the pedal needs replacing.
  3. FMI 3, 4: the connector under the pedal — water from shoes and dirt from the mat get in there. The wire chafes on the floor with every press.
  4. If the code comes together with SPN 29 and 558 — a shared connector or pedal supply.
  5. After replacing the pedal, some makes need the end positions taught.

Common causes — from most to least frequent

  1. Worn sensor in the pedal.
  2. Water and dirt in the connector under the pedal.
  3. Wire chafed at floor level.
  4. End positions not taught after replacement.

Can I keep driving

Do not drive. Stop as soon as it’s safe to do so — don’t keep going.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: To the nearest safe stop

The code is flagged critical: the manufacturer considers it unsafe to keep driving without checking.

This code alone cannot say what will fail next, so stop at the first safe opportunity and look at it on the spot.

That said, the manufacturer flags this code as critical — if in doubt, it is safer to stop and check on the spot.
