SPN 174 — Fuel temperature

Safe to driveTo the nearest safe stop

J1939 code SPN 174. Below — how the fault is worded at the factory by Foton, Isuzu, KAMAZ, MAZ, Renault Trucks, SITRAK, YaMZ and ZF AS-Tronic, and what the second half of the code (FMI) means in the standard.

How to check it yourself

The fuel temperature sensor sits on the fuel pump, the filter or the return line. Hot fuel is thinner: the ECU reduces delivery and the engine loses power.

What it should read

Resistance of a typical NTC sensor at 20 °Cabout 2…3 kΩ
The same at 80 °Cabout 300 Ω — more precisely per the factory table
Voltage at the harness connector with the sensor unpluggedabout 5 V

Check order

  1. FMI 0, 15, 16 (fuel hot): low fuel in the tank — the return warms a small volume faster. Top up and see whether the code goes away.
  2. Fuel cooler on the return (where fitted) clogged with dirt on the outside.
  3. A large leak-off from the injectors also heats the fuel — check the return.
  4. FMI 3, 4: unplug the connector and measure the sensor resistance. An open sensor or wire is seen by the ECU as −40 °C frost (voltage high, FMI 3), a short as severe overheating (FMI 4). With the connector unplugged, the harness contacts should show about 5 V.
  5. Compare the diagnostics reading on a cold engine that has stood overnight with other temperatures: air, oil, coolant. All should be close to the outside temperature; one lagging 10 °C or more is suspect.

Common causes — from most to least frequent

  1. Driving on a nearly empty tank.
  2. Dirty fuel cooler.
  3. Worn injectors with high leak-off.
  4. Connector or the sensor itself.

Can I keep driving

Safe to drive. Below — how much time you have and what happens if you keep going.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: To the nearest safe stop

Fuel is not arriving in the right amount or at the right pressure. It shows up first on climbs and under load.

You will most likely stall out on the road, and may not restart on the shoulder. A clogged filter or water in the tank you can nurse to a shop; a failing injection pump you cannot.

This is an electrical fault: an open, a short or a bad contact in the sensor circuit. The value it measures is most likely fine. You can drive. But until the circuit is repaired this system runs blind and cannot warn you about a real failure.

What to check on the spot

Check the fuel filter and water trap, drain the water. In cold weather, suspect fuel waxing.
